River paddeling

A slower, but interesting trip that makes it down the river Slush. We paddle for most of the time making it a less fast pace activity but more of a team-building excercise. We have three stops along the way with team-building exercises and one that also includes lunch. The trip lasts for about 4 hours.

White water rafting

The white water rafting is the more challenging experience. It takes you down the most wild parts of the river Bounty. The trip includes transport up the river, traning and instructions on how to use equipment and safety measures on the raft. The trip ends at our main site with team-building exercises. The trip lasts for about 4 hours.

Gatherings

We also offer to use our main camp as a site for gatherings of any kind. That can be a family gathering, a corporate retreat or other festivities and parties. We have a large dining venue that can seat 80 people and we can fix catering for any need. The gathering area is available every evening of the week from 18:00.
